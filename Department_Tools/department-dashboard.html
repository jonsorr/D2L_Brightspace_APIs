Courses listed in CONFIG.gradebookOnlyPrefixes show a link to the Gradebook instead of counts, and courses in CONFIG.passFailCourses use the Successful/Unsuccessful thresholds.
At risk 60–69%, not passing 59% or below. ENG-010 and ENG-011: at risk 70–74.9%, unsuccessful below 70%. Students with nothing graded yet are left out of both counts.
