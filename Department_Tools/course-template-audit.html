Course template audit

Checks the sections you're attached to in Brightspace for the required top-level content modules, and flags the ones still carrying modules that shouldn't be there. No course content is read until you press Run audit.

Checking your session…
What this report covers It audits the course sections your Brightspace account is attached to — nothing else. Your enrollments are read when the page loads and grouped into the terms they belong to; only the sections in the terms you pick get their content checked. It does not enumerate every section in a term, so a section you aren't enrolled in won't appear here, administrator or not.
