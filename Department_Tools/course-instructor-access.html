Instructor last access

For the sections you're attached to in Brightspace, reads each course's classlist and reports when every Faculty member last opened the course. No classlist is read until you press Run check.

Checking your session…
What this report covers It reads the classlists of the active course sections your Brightspace account is attached to — nothing else. Your enrollments are read when the page loads and grouped into the terms they belong to; inactive sections are skipped, and only the sections in the terms you pick get their classlists scanned. Note: reading a classlist counts as you visiting that course, so your own Last Accessed reflects the last time this tool (or you) opened the course — other instructors' dates are unaffected. It does not enumerate every section in a term, so a section you aren't attached to won't appear here, administrator or not.
